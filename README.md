# Credit Risk Decision Engine

An end-to-end credit underwriting and risk decisioning platform that simulates how a fintech lender can evaluate customer risk, estimate probability of default, apply underwriting policy, and measure the impact of lending decisions on portfolio growth and credit losses.

## Project Status

Phase 0 — Project Initialization

## Planned Components

- Synthetic financial data generation
- Behavioral cash-flow feature engineering
- Credit risk modeling
- Probability calibration
- MLflow experiment tracking
- Credit policy engine
- Business impact simulation
- Plaid Sandbox integration
- FastAPI decision service
- Streamlit risk dashboard
- Model monitoring and governance
