Model responsibility:
Estimate Probability of Default.

Policy responsibility:
Convert PD and operational constraints into a credit decision.

Policy tiers:
FULL_APPROVAL
LOW_AND_GROW
FALLBACK
REJECT

Economic metrics:
Expected Loss = PD × LGD × Exposure

Expected Profit =
Revenue
- Expected Loss
- Funding Cost
- Servicing Cost

Initial thresholds and economic assumptions are synthetic
portfolio assumptions and are subject to optimization.

The policy engine is versioned independently from the model.
